PRIVACY POLICY FOR DRENCHED

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Drenched is a hydration tracker for Android. This policy explains what the app
does and does not do with your information. The short version: there is no
account to sign up for, your hydration data is never sent to us, and the only
copy that ever leaves your phone is the one Android puts in your own Google
Drive backup - which you control and we cannot read.


WHAT DRENCHED STORES ON YOUR DEVICE
-----------------------------------

Everything you do in Drenched is saved locally on your phone, in the app's own
private storage:

  - Your drink log - when you logged a drink, how much, and what type
  - Your daily goal, cup size, and unit preference (ml or oz)
  - Your streak, XP, level, and unlocked achievements
  - Your cloud's name, appearance choices, and companion bond progress
  - Your reminder and notification settings

This data is never sent to us. There are no Drenched servers to send it to.
If you uninstall Drenched, the copy on your phone is deleted with it.


ANDROID BACKUP
--------------

Drenched takes part in Android's built-in backup, so that upgrading or replacing
your phone does not cost you your history and your streak. This is the only
circumstance in which your hydration data leaves your device.

To be exact about what that means:

  - The backup goes to YOUR Google Drive, under your Google account. It does
    not come to us, and we have no way to read it.
  - On Android 9 and later it is encrypted with your device PIN, pattern, or
    password before it leaves the phone.
  - It does not count against your Drive storage quota.
  - It covers your drink log and your settings. It does not, and cannot, include
    anything we do not already store on the device - see the list above.

You can switch it off at any time in Android Settings -> Google -> Backup, or
per-app on some devices. Drenched works exactly the same either way; you simply
start from scratch on a new phone.


WHAT DRENCHED DOES NOT DO
-------------------------

  - No account, sign-up, email address, or login
  - No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  - No analytics, tracking, profiling, or behavioural measurement
  - No selling or sharing of personal information with anyone
  - No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access


THE UPDATE CHECK
----------------

When Drenched starts, it fetches one small public file from GitHub to find out
whether the installed version is still supported. This is the only network
request the app makes on its own.

The request sends nothing about you: no hydration data, no settings, and no
identifier of any kind. As with any request to any website, GitHub can see the
IP address the request came from. That is unavoidable for any network request,
and it is not something Drenched collects, receives, stores, or has access to.

If the request fails for any reason, the app carries on exactly as normal.


PURCHASES
---------

Drenched offers one optional paid upgrade, "Max". The app is fully usable
without it and nothing is ever charged unless you choose to buy.

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing and by RevenueCat, which manages
subscription status on our behalf.

When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated
anonymous app user ID along with purchase and subscription details from Google
Play (such as which product was bought, and whether the subscription is active).
This is used solely to unlock and keep your Max features unlocked across
reinstalls. It is not linked to your name or email address by Drenched.

Google's handling of your payment details is governed by the Google Privacy
Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

RevenueCat's handling is governed by the RevenueCat Privacy Policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Drenched itself never sees or stores your payment card, billing address, or
Google account details.


PERMISSIONS DRENCHED REQUESTS
-----------------------------

  Internet
      Used for the version check described above, and to verify your purchase
      status with RevenueCat and Google Play. Your hydration data is never
      transmitted to us. (Android's own backup, described above, is handled by
      the operating system and not by this permission.)

  Notifications
      Used to send you hydration reminders and your optional bedtime reminder.
      You can decline this and use the app normally, or turn reminders off at
      any time in Settings.

  Vibrate
      Used for reminder alerts and for the tactile feedback when you press a
      button.

  Run at startup
      Used only to re-schedule your reminders after the phone restarts, so they
      keep working without you having to open the app.


A NOTE ON HEALTH
----------------

Drenched estimates a daily water goal from details you enter, and it warns you
when you drink well past that goal. These are estimates and general guidance
only. Drenched is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. If
you have a health condition affecting how much you should drink, follow your
doctor rather than this app.


CHILDREN
--------

Drenched is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets
a higher age for consent to online services, as some EU countries do), and we do
not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only information
tied to any user is the anonymous purchase ID described above.


YOUR CONTROL OVER YOUR DATA
---------------------------

All of your data lives on your device, so you are in full control of it:

  - Turn off any reminder or notification in Settings
  - Turn off Android backup in Android Settings -> Google -> Backup
  - Uninstalling the app deletes every copy of your Drenched data on the phone.
    If Android backup is on, a copy remains in your own Google Drive until you
    delete it there or Android expires it


FOR USERS IN THE EU, EEA, UK AND SWITZERLAND
--------------------------------------------

This section applies if you are in the European Union, the European Economic
Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, and explains how Drenched meets the
General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

Who is responsible
    The developer of Drenched, as named on the Drenched Google Play store page
    and reachable at the contact address at the end of this policy, is the
    controller of the small amount of personal data described here.

What personal data is involved, and why
    Your hydration log, goals and settings stay on your device and are never
    sent to us, so we do not process them. The personal data that does leave
    your device is:

  - The anonymous app user ID and purchase details sent to RevenueCat when you
    buy or restore Max. Legal basis: performance of a contract (delivering the
    upgrade you paid for).
  - The IP address GitHub sees when the app checks for updates. Legal basis:
    legitimate interest in making sure you are not running a version that no
    longer works. Drenched does not receive or store this address.

    Android backup to your Google Drive is a service between you and Google,
    which you can switch off at any time.

International transfers
    Google, RevenueCat and GitHub are based in the United States. Transfers of
    personal data to them rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the
    European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long data is kept
    Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat and Google for as long as needed to
    keep your purchase working and to meet their legal and tax obligations.
    Everything else is kept only on your device, until you delete it or uninstall
    the app.

Your rights
    You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the
    processing of your personal data, and to receive it in a portable format.
    Because we hold no hydration data and cannot link a purchase ID to you,
    please include your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") if you
    want us to act on purchase data. We will reply within one month.

    You also have the right to complain to your local data protection
    authority. A list of EU authorities is at https://edpb.europa.eu

No automated decisions
    Drenched does not make any automated decisions that have legal or similarly
    significant effects on you. The daily water goal is a suggestion that you
    can change at any time.
