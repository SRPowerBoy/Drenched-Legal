Privacy Policy for Drenched
Last updated: 24 July 2026

Drenched is a hydration tracker for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: your hydration data never leaves your phone, and there is no account to sign up for.

What Drenched stores on your device
Everything you do in Drenched is saved locally on your phone, in the app's own private storage:

Your drink log — when you logged a drink, how much, and what type
Your daily goal, cup size, and unit preference (ml or oz)
Your streak, XP, level, and unlocked achievements
Your cloud's name, appearance choices, and companion bond progress
Your reminder and notification settings
This data is not uploaded anywhere. It is not backed up to our servers, because there are no servers. If you uninstall Drenched, this data is deleted with it.

What Drenched does not do
No account, sign-up, email address, or login
No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
No analytics, tracking, profiling, or behavioural measurement
No selling or sharing of personal information with anyone
No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
Purchases
Drenched offers an optional paid upgrade ("Max"). Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing and by RevenueCat, which manages subscription status on our behalf.

When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous app user ID along with purchase and subscription details from Google Play (such as which product was bought, and whether the subscription is active). This is used solely to unlock and keep your Max features unlocked across reinstalls. It is not linked to your name or email address by Drenched.

Google's handling of your payment details is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. RevenueCat's handling is governed by the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Drenched itself never sees or stores your payment card, billing address, or Google account details.

Permissions Drenched requests
Internet — used only to verify your purchase status with RevenueCat and Google Play. Your hydration data is never transmitted.
Notifications — used to send you hydration reminders, drink-ready alerts, and your optional bedtime reminder. You can decline this and use the app normally, or turn reminders off at any time in Settings.
Vibrate — used for reminder alerts and for the tactile feedback when you press a button.
Alarms / exact alarms — used only to deliver your bedtime reminder at the time you chose.
Children
Drenched is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to delete on request.

Your control over your data
All of your data lives on your device, so you are in full control of it:

Turn off any reminder or notification in Settings
Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of your Drenched data
Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
