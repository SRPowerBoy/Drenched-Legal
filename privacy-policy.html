Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2025

Drenched does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. Drenched stores your hydration logs, settings, and preferences exclusively in local storage on your device (Android Room database and DataStore). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to any server.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app saves the following information only on your device:

  • Water intake logs (cups logged and timestamps)
  • Daily hydration goal and cup size preference
  • Notification and reminder settings
  • Customization choices (themes, cloud styles, progress styles)
  • Gamification data (XP, streaks, unlocked items)
  • Subscription status (managed by Google Play)

You can delete all of this at any time by uninstalling the app or using the data wipe option in Settings.

Internet & Billing

Drenched uses the Google Play Billing Library solely to process subscription and one-time purchase transactions. All payment handling is performed entirely by Google Play — we never see, store, or process your payment information. Google Play's own privacy policy governs that interaction.

Beyond billing, the app makes no network requests and does not connect to any server operated by us.

Analytics & Advertising

We do not use analytics services, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking tools. There are no ads in Drenched.

Children's Privacy

Drenched does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. The app is a general wellness tool with no account creation, no social features, and no data transmission.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any data to third parties — because we do not collect any data in the first place.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times. To remove all app data, uninstall Drenched or use Settings → Wipe All Data (debug builds) within the app.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices — for example, if we add analytics or cloud sync — we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before shipping that change.
